QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before we get started.

Have a specific request? Send it to our team and we will help clarify the next steps.

Ask a question →
Which products can I enquire about?

You can enquire about ceramic, porcelain and vitrified tiles, marble, granite and selected interior products. Share the required product, size, finish and quantity so availability can be checked.

Can I request a bulk or project quotation?

Yes. Include your estimated order quantity, destination country, product specification and any target delivery requirements. Pricing and minimum quantities are confirmed individually.

Do you support international shipping?

Grey Zen can coordinate export-related requirements with you. Shipping route, freight, documentation, Incoterms and lead time must be confirmed for the specific order and destination.

Can I request samples or a product catalogue?

Mention samples or catalogue access in your enquiry. Options and availability depend on the product category and the current sourcing arrangement.

How do I get a quote faster?

Include your name, business, destination country, quantity, preferred dimensions or finish and a target timeline. The more specific the request, the easier it is to assess.
